2 · Ground rules

  • DiscoverDon't assume the stack. Surdej is described as Fastify, Prisma, Postgres, NATS on Azure Container Apps; confirm against surdej-v2 and follow what is actually there.
  • TokensPort tokens.json to the app's theme (CSS variables, light and [data-theme="dark"]). Customer portal is light; backoffice is dark. No raw hex in components.
  • ComponentsRebuild the five design-system components with the same props: Button (variant, size, href), Badge (tone, dot), Input (label, hint, error), Stat (label, value, unit, delta, trend), ServiceCard (line, eyebrow, title, specs, price, cta). Then lift the repeated shells from proto.css: top bar, side nav, card, table, tabs.
  • CopyDanish copy comes verbatim from the artboards. Dates are DD.MM.YYYY, numbers use Danish separators (12.480 · 3,8).
  • Mock dataNames and figures in the screens are demo data. They belong in a seed script, never in components.
  • A11yKeep the semantics the screens already have: real buttons and links, labelled inputs, aria-labels on icon buttons, tables that scroll inside their box on phones.

4 · Domain model read from the screens

Tenancy and access

  • OrganizationCVR, billing address, EAN, require-2FA flag
  • User · Membershipcustomer roles: kundeadministrator, kundebruger (location scope, may order); staff roles: administrator, analytiker, pilot, kundeansvarlig
  • Invitationexpires after 7 days, resend
  • ShareLinkfor report or location; expiry, optional code, revocable
  • AuditLogincl. “Vis som kunde” impersonation and report publishing

Customer side

  • Locationtype bygning · byggeplads · område; address, geometry, site contact, access notes
  • Order (B-1042)tilbud sendt → afventer godkendelse → planlagt → vejrforbehold → leveret; cancel until 24 h before
  • Quote · Subscriptionfrequency, number of visits, binding period
  • Invoice · InvoiceDraftF-2026-0311; ubetalt / betalt; drafts klar / afventer levering
  • Servicecatalogue: industry, equipment, permit class (A1/A2/A3/BVLOS), price unit
  • SupportCase · LeadS-118; leads from the website form

Operations

  • Task (OP-2026-0418)phases Forberedelse → Flyvning → Data → Leverance; pilot, zone check, weather, checklist
  • Flight (FL-0931)drone, batteries, duration, wind, incidents
  • Equipmentdrone, battery cycles, next service; status klar / til kontrol / til service
  • ComplianceDocumentoperator registration, insurance, pilot certificates; expiry date

Data and AI

  • Upload · MediaAssetJPG, DNG, MP4, MOV, SRT; resumable, up to 20 GB
  • ProcessingJob (J-5518)step: sløring, fotogrammetri, AI-analyse; i kø / kører / fejlet / færdig; error text; restart
  • AIModel · ModelVersionplanlagt / test / i drift; test precision; corrections since last version
  • Findingtype, severity (kritisk · moderat · lav), confidence, model version, image and position, QC state, customer status (ny · planlagt · udbedret · afvist), owner, change since last visit
  • Report · ReportTemplateversioned, published by, PDF, data download
  • CmsPagelanguages, kladde / udgivet

5 · Rules the screens imply

RuleWhere
People and number plates are blurred before images are stored or shown. No personal images are kept from events, only anonymous density.KP-Upload, AI-Event, KP-Hjaelp
A finding reaches the customer only after an analyst has approved it. The analyst who flew the task can't approve it (four-eyes).BO-Kvalitet, KP-Fund
Every finding stores model version, confidence and approver. QC corrections are saved as training data.BO-AI, BO-Kvalitet
A task can't be marked ready until every checklist item is done, including the zone permit when the location is in a restricted zone.BO-Opgave
Wind over 10 m/s or precipitation means vejrforbehold. The customer is told by the day before at the latest.BO-Opgave, KP-Bestilling
Equipment with status til kontrol or til service can't be picked in a checklist.BO-Udstyr
Compliance documents warn ahead of expiry (shown at 26 days) and appear on the backoffice overview.BO-Compliance, BO-Overblik
Uploads run automatic checks (GPS, overlap ≥ 70 %, altitude, blur) before analysis can start.KP-Upload, BO-Data
Pilot upload is resumable and can pause; video can wait for wifi. A post-flight checklist closes the flight.BO-Upload
Only customer admins see invoices. An organization can require 2FA for all users.KP-Fakturaer, KP-Brugere
Customer users can be limited to selected locations and to ordering or not.KP-Brugere
Staff impersonation (“Vis som kunde”) is written to the audit log.BO-Kunder, BO-Admin

6 · Phases and acceptance

P0 · Foundations
  • PLAN.md approved. Theme tokens, the five components and the two app shells (portal light, backoffice dark) in place.
  • Login with email and password plus Microsoft and Google; organizations, memberships, roles, invitations, 2FA policy.
  • Done when: a customer user and a staff user each land on their own empty shell with the right nav and theme.
P1 · Customer core
  • Lokationer (list, filters), Lokation (timeline, tabs), Bestillinger, the four-step Ny bestilling wizard, quotes, Overblik.
  • Done when: Mette (kundeadministrator) can create a location, order an inspection, approve a quote and see it on the overview.
P2 · Operations
  • Opgaver (week calendar, list, map), Opgave preparation (zone check, weather, checklist), Udstyr, Flyvelog og compliance, backoffice Overblik.
  • Weather and drone-zone data behind an interface with a stub provider.
  • Done when: an order becomes a task, the checklist gates “Markér klar”, and a logged flight shows in the logbook.
P3 · Data pipeline
  • Resumable upload (pilot mobile and customer), automatic checks, jobs on NATS: blur → photogrammetry → AI analysis. Databehandling, AI-modeller registry, Kvalitetskontrol.
  • Models are adapters with a stub that returns fixture findings; no training in scope.
  • Done when: uploaded photos produce a job chain, a failed job shows its error and can restart, and an analyst can approve, correct or reject each finding.
P4 · Delivery
  • Report templates, generate and publish (PDF via Surdej's document pipeline), versioning, share links. Portal Rapporter, Fund and the six analysis report views driven by one report schema.
  • Done when: a published report appears for the customer with only approved findings, and a share link expires and can be revoked.
P5 · Business and content
  • Kunder and leads, Økonomi (drafts, service catalogue), portal Fakturaer, Organisation settings, Hjælp and support cases, Indhold (CMS), public website, Administration with audit log and integrations.
  • Accounting system behind an interface; export drafts only until one is chosen.
  • Done when: a website lead becomes a customer with a quote, and a delivered task becomes an invoice draft.

7 · Out of scope for now

  • Training or hosting real AI models (stubs and adapters only)
  • Real accounting, weather or drone-zone integrations (interfaces and stubs)
  • 3D viewer and map rendering beyond a placeholder component with a defined input
  • ISO-XML, shapefile and O&M exports from the analysis views (buttons present, disabled)
